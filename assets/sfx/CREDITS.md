# Sound effect credits

All sound effects are released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**, so they may be redistributed freely.
Each was converted with macOS `afconvert` to 16-bit PCM WAV, mono, 22.05 kHz (`afconvert -f WAVE -d LEI16@22050 -c 1`).

## Source packs

1. **The Essential Retro Video Game Sound Effects Collection [512 sounds]** by **Juhani Junkala**
   (uploaded as "512 Sound Effects (8-bit style)" by SubspaceAudio), CC0.
   <https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style>
   (license stated on the page and in the pack's `INFO.txt`: CC0, no attribution required).
2. **Digital Audio / "63 digital sound effects (lasers, phasers, space, etc.)"** by **Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)**, CC0.
   <https://opengameart.org/content/63-digital-sound-effects-lasers-phasers-space-etc>
   (license stated on the page: CC0; the pack's readme: "You may use these sounds in personal and commercial projects").
   The originals are MP3.

## Synthesised interface sounds (CC0)

`ui_click`, `ui_hover`, `ui_select`, `tower_place`, `tower_upgrade`, `tower_sell` and `invalid` are generated by
[`python/lindefense/tools/sfx_synth.py`](../../python/lindefense/tools/sfx_synth.py) (sine / triangle oscillators, pitch
sweeps, light FM, short envelopes, soft echo and low-pass, a touch of bit-crush). They are works created for
this project and are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**. Regenerate them with
`cd python && .venv/bin/python -m lindefense.tools.sfx_synth ../assets/sfx`.
