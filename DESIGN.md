| MOAB-class | any of the 5 bosses |

| Status effects | SLOW: strongest applies, never stacks, bosses get 1/3 of any slow. STUN/FREEZE: bosses immune unless the tower says otherwise. | Buff stacking | same source tower type never stacks (highest wins); different types add. Caps: +100% attack speed, +10 pierce, +5 flat damage |

| Knockback budget | per enemy, pushes from attacks are capped per second at 40% (bosses 25%) of the enemy's unslowed movement, so stacked pushers can never hold an enemy in place; ability blasts and scheduled events ignore the cap |
| Rewards | each body pays its own reward exactly once when destroyed; leaks pay nothing; no double payment on splits |
| Lives | leaked enemy removes its listed lives (a leaking Bucket Sort also leaks everything it stored); 0 lives = defeat |
| Victory | destroying Palantir's body on wave 100 (5 s defeat sequence, then victory screen) |
| Targeting | First (closest to exit), Last, Strongest (most HP), Weakest, Closest (to tower); Densest Cluster (Grok U2+): the enemy with the most other enemies within 60 px, ties go to First |
| Line of sight | only The Legacy Monolith's server chassis block it |

## 2. Towers

The tower shop sells 18 towers. The 17 original towers have one base form and **exactly three** purchasable upgrades
(tier 0-3). Every shop tower has an overpowered active ability that is **locked until the tower is fully upgraded and the player pays $15000** for it (U or the side-panel button); it is ready the moment it is bought. **Zethos** (Primary)
has **four** upgrades (its Reality Collapse unlocks after the fourth); it is tuned to about twice the best of Gemini, Claude
and GPT at each tier. The 18 **heroes** are not in the tower shop: they are unlocked once in the **Hero Store** (permanent,
saved between sessions), then pay a deploy cost once per match (at most 3 different heroes per game) and level up
through three purchasable upgrades; they have no active abilities. Mr. Lindemann is the strongest hero and Spear Hanbo the
second strongest. Heroes and Zethos are specified
in map units: 1 unit = 4 px (tables below are in px). Source of truth for every number: `lindefense/tower/data/*.java`.

#### Lobber Luis - Explosive Artillery (Heroes, Hero Store $1250)

Hero. Lobs an arcing explosive that deals full damage to everything in the blast. Slow, long-ranged and built for dense groups; later splits into cluster bombs and finally calls orbital strikes.

| 3 | Orbital Artillery | $1500 | 100 | Explosive | 2.5s | 220 | 1 | projectile 160 px/s, splash 88; knockback 10 |

- **U1 Heavy Payload** ($400): Heavy Payload: Damage 34 -> 45 and blast radius 12 -> 15 units; a heavier shell that also shoves survivors back.

- **U3 Orbital Artillery** ($1500): Damage 45 -> 100, blast radius 15 -> 22 units, and every 5th shot lands as a double-damage orbital strike with a shockwave.
- No active ability (passive).

## 7. Difficulty Growth

Four selectable modes (`lindefense.sim.Difficulty`; pick on the map-select screen or `-Dlindefense.difficulty=O1|ON|ON2|ONN`).
All knobs are relative multipliers on top of the base game. Ramped knobs use `base + (final - base) * growth(wave)` where
growth is 0 (O(1)), `w/100` (O(N)) or `(w/100)^2` (O(N^2) and O(N^N)).

| Knob | O(1) | O(N) | O(N^2) | O(N^N) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subtitle | Constant (current) | Linear (Normal) | Quadratic (Hard) | Super-exponential (Impossible) |
| Growth curve | flat | linear | quadratic | quadratic |
| Enemy HP x (wave 1 -> 100) | 1 -> 1 | 1.1 -> 1.5 | 1.1 -> 1.85 | 1.25 -> 3.1 |
| Enemy count x (wave 1 -> 100) | 1 -> 1 | 1.1 -> 1.4 | 1.1 -> 1.6 | 1.25 -> 2.4 |
| Tower damage x (flat) | 1 | 0.92 | 0.9 | 0.84 |
| Income x (kill rewards + wave bonus, flat) | 1 | 0.8 | 0.72 | 0.56 |
| Elite chance per spawn (wave 1 -> 100) | - | - | 2% -> 22% | 3% -> 80% |
| Elite extra modifiers | - | - | 1 of Camo/Regrow/Fortified | 2 of Camo/Regrow/Fortified |
| Elite promotion to tougher type | - | - | 20% of elites | 50% of elites |
| Palantir HP extra | x1 | x1.03 | x1 | x1.06 |
| Starting cash x / lives x | 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 |

Elites: each non-boss spawn is an elite with the chance above (deterministic from game seed + wave + group). Elites are split into their own
spawn group with extra random modifiers and, for some, a promotion to a tougher type (e.g. Quick Sort -> Heap Sort). Enemy counts are
rounded stochastically (deterministic per seed, minimum 1) and spawn intervals shrink by `1 + 0.5 (countMult - 1)`. Bosses already in the wave are never multiplied.
Palantir does not receive the ramped HP multiplier: its HP is multiplied by `towerDamage x palantirExtra` (row above) so the final fight takes about as long as in O(1) (a DPS check, not a wall).
Kill rewards and the wave bonus are both scaled by the income multiplier (research-tower income is not). Any mode can be tried with `-Dlindefense.difficulty=O1|ON|ON2|ONN`.

boss-damage core, focus fire on boss waves, abilities when they matter) on the four original maps (Silicon Circuit, Legacy Monolith,
Hallucination Canyon, Singularity Core) x seeds 1234-1236, with prompt injection and Palantir's phase abilities active and Patch Notes off.

## 8. Prompt injection and Palantir's protocols

**Prompt injection** (`sim/PromptInjection`, schedule in `wave/InjectionSchedule`): an injector carries the sign "IGNORE PREVIOUS INSTRUCTIONS". It broadcasts when a tower comes within 150 px and every 9 s after, hijacking the 2 nearest non-immune towers for 3 s. A hijacked tower stops attacking and zaps a neighbour within 170 px offline for 1.5 s, 2 times per hijack. Chains are one hop and every outage is followed by a short protection window.
